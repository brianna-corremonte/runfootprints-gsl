Footprints · Great Salt Lake

The Great Salt Lake is disappearing beneath our feet.

We're running the full 256-mile perimeter of the lake over 5 days to raise awareness while there's still time to act. Along the way, this map stops at the places with real stories to tell, and the organizations already doing the work. Scroll down to follow the route and see what's at stake at each one.
